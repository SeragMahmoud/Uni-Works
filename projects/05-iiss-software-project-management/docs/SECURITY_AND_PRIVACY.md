# Security And Privacy

## Public Boundary

Original Office files are not included because they may contain:

- Author metadata.
- Internal comments.
- Private schedule assumptions.
- Team/student information.
- Reviewer or faculty context not intended for GitHub.

## Safe Publication Practices

- Export only reviewed, sanitized summaries.
- Avoid local absolute paths.
- Avoid emails, phone numbers, student IDs, or private group information.
- Keep private schedules and sign-off material out of public commits unless explicitly approved.
